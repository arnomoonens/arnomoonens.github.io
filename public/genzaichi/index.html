How it works

  1. Photograph the map board.Straight on is best. Any photo from your library works too.
  2. Pin two spots.Standing at the board? Put the crosshair on its 現在地 (You are here) mark and tap I'm here. Then line up one more spot with the satellite map.
  3. Walk.Your red dot moves across the photo. Add a spot whenever the dot drifts, and the fit gets better.
